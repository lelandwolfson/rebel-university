# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please report privately. Do not open a public issue.

- Preferred: GitHub private vulnerability reporting, at the "Report a vulnerability" button on this repository's **Security** tab.
- The current contact is always listed at <https://rebeluniversity.org/.well-known/security.txt>.

Include what you found, how to reproduce it, and what an attacker could get. We aim to reply within 7 days and to fix serious issues within 30.

## Scope

In scope: the code in this repository and the site at rebeluniversity.org.

Out of scope: volumetric denial of service, social engineering, reports from automated scanners without a demonstrated impact, and the third-party video services that organizers link to.

Please don't access data that isn't yours, don't degrade the service for others, and give us reasonable time to fix before disclosure. Good-faith research under these terms is welcome, and we won't pursue it.
